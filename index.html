  1. 01

    Suit up

    You put on a fitted vest, belt and cuffs with electrodes over a base layer. A little water on the electrodes keeps the contact steady.

  2. 02

    Train

    Your trainer raises the intensity step by step while you do slow movements: squats, rows, lunges. No weights needed.

  3. 03

    Recover

    The impulses stop and you cool down. Expect tired muscles, like after a hard workout. Leave 48 hours before your next EMS session.

Who it suits

A good fit if you

  • have little time and want a full workout in one short slot
  • surf, run or dive and want strength work without heavy loading on the joints
  • are coming back to training and want a guided, controlled start
  • sit for most of the day and want to wake up your back and core

Before you book

EMS is not suitable if you have

  • a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device
  • pregnancy
  • epilepsy
  • a serious heart or circulation condition, or uncontrolled high blood pressure
  • a fever or acute infection
  • open wounds or skin problems where the electrodes sit

If in doubt, ask your doctor first, and tell your trainer about any condition or medication.

Questions

Before your first time

Does it hurt?

No. You feel a strong tingling and your muscles contract. Your trainer sets the intensity to what you can handle and changes it as you go.

How often should I train?

Most people start with one session a week and always keep at least 48 hours between sessions, so the muscles can recover.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable, fitted sportswear and a bottle of water. Ask us when you book what the studio provides.

Will I see results?

Results depend on consistency, food and sleep. EMS adds to a healthy routine; it does not replace one.
